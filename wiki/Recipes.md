# Recipes

Short answers to the things people actually come here to do.

---

**Find the coordinates of a structure**
→ Type its abbreviation or name, click the result, read the **Label centre** row on the
card. See [Finding Structures](Finding-Structures).

---

**Read from lambda instead of bregma**
→ **Frame** → *Origin* → *Zero the readout on* → **lambda** → **Turn on**. Every readout in
the app switches over and says `lambda`, not `AP`. See [Working Frame](Working-Frame).

---

**See a whole pathway at once**
→ Click the **auditory** chip (or whichever system), then open the **Projection** view. The
whole system's labels are plotted in AP × DV. See
[Projection and 3D](Projection-and-3D).

---

**See what is at a coordinate I already have**
→ Left panel → **At a coordinate**, type AP / ML / DV, read the ranked list. Or hit
**Pick on the plate** and click where you are aiming. See
[At a Coordinate](At-a-Coordinate).

---

**Check that a structure's drawn boundary matches the real tissue**
→ Select it, then switch the plate between **Labeled** and **Nissl**. The circle stays
put, because all three sources are registered. See [Reading a Plate](Reading-a-Plate).

---

**Get the approach angle between two points**
→ Tick **Measure**, click both points. The readout gives the distance, the ML and DV
components, and the angle from vertical. See [Measuring](Measuring).

---

**Plan an electrode track**
→ Left panel → **Plan a track**, pick the target and hemisphere, set your angles, read
**Entry** and **Drive**. Set your [working frame](Working-Frame) *first* if your head is
tilted — the angles are in your frame. See [Planning a Track](Planning-a-Track).

---

**Compare a tilted approach to a straight one**
→ In the planner, the **Straight down** row always shows what the drive would be on a
vertical dorsal approach, alongside your angled one.

---

**Make a figure**
→ Set up the plate — source, grey, contrast, overlays — then **SVG** for editable
outlines, or **PNG** if you want the histology in it. See [Exporting](Exporting).

---

**Get every auditory structure into a spreadsheet**
→ Click the **auditory** chip, then **CSV**. It exports exactly what is listed. See
[Exporting](Exporting).

---

**Send someone the exact view I'm looking at**
→ **Copy link**. It carries the plate, structure, zoom, overlays, source, view, 3D
settings, planned track and frame.

---

**Work on a rig computer with no internet**
→ Download `gerbil_atlas_explorer.html` and double-click it. Everything is inside that one
file, links included.

---

**Browse a level structure by structure**
→ Set the scope strip to **On plate N**. The list now follows the plate slider, so walking
the slider walks the anatomy.

---

**Find which plate a bregma value lands on**
→ Type `bregma -2.3` into the search box. It jumps to the nearest plate.
